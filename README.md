
# InFoo

InFoo is a desktop application built using JavaFX, designed to provide football enthusiasts with all the essential information about their favorite players and clubs. Whether you're looking for player stats, club histories, or recent updates, INfoo offers a user-friendly platform that brings everything football-related to your fingertips. With its sleek design and intuitive interface, INfoo ensures you stay connected to the world of football effortlessly. Whether you're a casual fan or a die-hard follower, INfoo has got you covered with detailed insights and up-to-date data.


## Features

- Players List
    - Players Information
- Search Players
- Clubs List
    - Clubs Information
- Search Clubs
- Players Comparison
- Hall of Fames
- Previous seasons summary
